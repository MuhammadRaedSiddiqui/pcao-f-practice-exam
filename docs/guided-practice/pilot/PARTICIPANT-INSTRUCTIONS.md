This is a test of the application, not an assessment of your intelligence or ability. We want to understand whether its questions, hints, feedback and controls are clear to people preparing for PCAO-F. Your answers will not predict certification performance.

Taking part is voluntary. You may skip a prompt, take a break or stop at any time without giving a reason. Please do not include personal, work-confidential or certification-record information in your explanations. The facilitator will explain the approved consent, notes and withdrawal arrangements before you choose whether to take part.

1. Open the local Guided Practice page the facilitator provides. No registration is needed. Select **5 questions** and your assigned Practice set, then select **Start practice**.
2. Read the scenario and options at your own pace. Explain your reasoning naturally: what decision is needed, what information matters, and which other option seems plausible. There is no timer or pressure to be correct.
3. Some questions require one answer, others two or three. Follow **Select exactly …**. You can change an unchecked answer or clear it. Use **Next** and **Previous** to move around.
4. Use hints whenever useful. Each **Reveal hint** shows the next hint, up to three. Say what it helped you understand, or if it confused you. You do not have to request hints.
5. When ready, select **Check answer**. Your first saved checked answer becomes locked for this session. Feedback then explains the reasoning, each option, vocabulary and sources. Say what the explanation means in your own words and what is unclear. You may disagree with the explanation.
6. With the facilitator, move away and back once and reload once to check that your progress returns. After checking all five answers, select **Finish practice** and inspect the read-only summary.

Tell us whenever wording, instructions, tables, controls or feedback are confusing, or saving/navigation behaves unexpectedly. The facilitator can help with operating the app but will not coach you toward a correct answer.

Plan for about **30–45 minutes**, including conversation and breaks. Progress is stored only in this browser; browser data clearing or storage eviction may remove it. There is no Guided cloud backup. Questions may already be familiar, and hints/feedback expose answers; the summary does not establish mastery or certification readiness.
