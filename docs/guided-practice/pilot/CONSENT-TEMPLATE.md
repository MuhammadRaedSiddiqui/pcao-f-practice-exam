We are checking whether the PCAO-F Guided Practice app's questions, hints, explanations and controls are understandable. This is a test of the application, not of your intelligence or ability. It does not predict certification results or prove that the app improves learning.

You will try one five-question local session and talk about your reasoning for approximately 30–45 minutes. You may use hints, take breaks, decline a prompt or stop at any time without giving a reason or penalty. Tell the facilitator if wording, feedback or controls are confusing. Do not share personal, confidential work or certification-record information.

The facilitator will record a pseudonym, consent reference, date/set/artifact, task reasoning, hints used, feedback understanding, control/saving difficulties, assistance, defects and comments. Notes may include short quotes only with your permission below. Answers are saved locally in the dedicated browser profile while it is retained. No account signup, learner telemetry or cloud backup is used. **No audio or video recording is planned.** Any later recording proposal needs separate owner approval and explicit optional consent; participation cannot depend on recording.
